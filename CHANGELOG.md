# Changelog

## 0.4.0
- **Merchants**: currencies and prices for Item Piles and the merchant stock generator are now part of the GM Suite, with a new tab. Item Piles is optional.
- **Chat command hints** for Chat Commander are now part of the GM Suite. Chat Commander is optional.
- The quick tests on the Token HUD moved to **Warhammer Battle HUD**.

## 0.3.1
- WFRP: vehicles can take damage on the Combat tab (select their tokens).

## 0.3.0
- **Light & Vision** tab: light and vision presets for the selected tokens, with a custom range.
- **Dark Whispers** tab (systems with Corruption): a temptation whispered to the player, who answers on the chat card. Obeying can remove 1 Corruption automatically.
- **Token HUD**: quick tests with their values for the owner of the token.

## 0.2.0
- **Combat** tab: apply damage (a number or a dice formula) to several characters with the system's own rules, with one summary for the GM.
- Combat pools where the system has them: WFRP Advantage (per character or the Group Advantage rule), Imperium Maledictum Superiority, Wrath & Glory Glory and Ruin.

## 0.1.0
- First version: one GM dashboard that detects the active Warhammer system (WFRP 4e/5e, The Old World, Imperium Maledictum, Wrath & Glory, Soulbound).
- **Session** tab: award XP with a reason, restore the system's resource (Fortune, Fate, Wrath, Soulfire), end-of-session routine (chat export and clearing, XP, resource, holding scene, session number, summary).
- **Group Test** tab: secret tests for several characters with the system's own skills, difficulty and roll dialog, and one summary for the GM.
- Each tab can be switched off in the settings.
- Tested with Foundry v14 and WFRP4e 10.1.2 (4e rules).
